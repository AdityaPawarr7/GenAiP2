01

Expectations vs. reality

Going into this project, I was expecting it to be an easy 2 hour sprint where I gather data and then Claude does the rest for me as that's how I approach vibe coding but gosh it was so much different than I could have imagined. My approach to vibe coding is having a final target layout and then building towards it. I don't usually build with principles and groundwork laid out first and then following that to go to a final product. This approach, however- does have its benefits. I noticed that because of how detailed my Acceptance Criteria was, the output that Claude provided me with at the end had an amazing amount of data and trends that we can draw from it. On the flip side, we did end up using a 147M tokens which is a lot for this as I ran out of limits once as well.

Research

It costs nothing, and it means the next task doesn’t keep paying for the previous one’s history.” Anthropic, Best practices for Claude Code

02

Detailed criteria, and 700+ tests

The final product was a culmination of me going back and forth with Claude and the detailed criteria resulted in Claude writing 700+ tests. This number to me feels unnecessary as it started writing tests for everything that could go wrong which I feel is hoping for the worst kind of mindset but it is a lot of bloat and tokens wasted too.

04

The UI still looks “AI-like”

I feel that the UI aspect of Claude is something that needs to be personalized to a user's personality and I tried importing a skill to help with that but my UI ended up being pretty 'AI- like'

Research

“this generic aesthetic undermines brand identity and makes AI-generated interfaces immediately recognizable—and dismissible.” Anthropic, Improving frontend design through Skills

05

What the findings say about school

My final product is a great example of how change is needed in this space for how AI is being used in school as with this technology- either we need rethink the entire system of education or have guard rails in place to use it efficiently. Because seeing it in black and white is dangerous- not using AI at all is silly as you're rejecting the advancement of technology and abstraction but at the flip side- you don't want to use AI when you don't know the concepts at all.

From the findings

4 of 42

32 of 42
leave the rule to each instructor; 8 ban AI by default and 0 encourage it

Research

“when AI assistance was taken away from the Base group and they were given an exam on the material, they performed 17% worse than the control group.” Knowledge at Wharton on Bastani et al., PNAS 2025: a guarded “GPT Tutor” largely avoided the harm “thought they performed better than they did, and finished with an illusion of competence.” Prather et al., The Widening Gap, ICER 2024: novice programmers using GenAI “Brain-to-LLM users exhibited higher memory recall” than students who had used the LLM from the start. Kosmyna et al., MIT Media Lab, Your Brain on ChatGPT (preprint, 2025)

Further reading

  1. Measuring the Impact of Early-2025 AI on Experienced Open-Source Developer Productivity METR, 2025. Developers expected AI to speed them up but took 19% longer.
