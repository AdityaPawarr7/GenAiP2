> Has ASU considered a ban on AI tools like other institutions such as NYU? No. ASU faculty and administrators are focused on the positive potential of generative AI while also thinking through concerns about ethics, academic integrity and privacy.

> Use of AI tools is generally welcome and even encouraged in this class. However, it is expected that all assignment submissions in this class are authored by the student , and not AI tools .

> AI tools are permitted to be employed to assist in debugging common code errors, provide additional clarification of course concepts, and generally serve as a tutor.
