## university_guidance
url: https://www.umass.edu/studentsuccess/guidance-generative-artificial-intelligence
source_date: undated
> Students cannot use Generative AI (chatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, etc.) to complete any academic work without instructor permission. If an instructor has not given explicit permission to use Generative AI, students should assume they are not allowed to do so in that course.

> Instructors should have clear expectations on Generative AI use in their courses, available to students in their course syllabus.

> The Academic Integrity Office does not recommend relying on detection tool reports when addressing suspected academic misconduct.

url: https://www.umass.edu/ctl/resources/generative-ai-course-policy-toolkit
source_date: undated
> The current Faculty Senate Policy is that students' unauthorized use of generative AI (GenAI) technologies for completing course work is prohibited under the Academic Honesty Policy. Instructors who allow students to use GenAI tools in their courses must explicitly inform students what uses of GenAI are permitted. The Faculty Senate strongly recommends that happen in a GenAI Use Statement on the syllabus.

## se_course
course: COMPSCI 320 Introduction to Software Engineering (section 1, James Perretta), Fall 2026
url: https://people.cs.umass.edu/~jperretta/cs320/f26/SYLLABUS.html
source_date: 2026 "Fall 2026 Syllabus - Revised 09/07/2026"
> This policy aims to guide students towards AI usage that allows them to explore the capabilities and trade-offs of using these tools without inadvertently preventing them from learning critical software engineering skills.

> MAY NOT : Use generative AI tools to aid in designing, documenting, or testing any code, features, or other contributions to your team's project. This includes diagrams. Use generative AI tools in any way to aid in the completion of homework assignments or presentation material.

> MAY : Use generative AI tools to write non-test code, set up infrastructure, or aid in other dev-ops tasks.

> Be able to explain without assistance any code or deliverables that you contribute or create a pull request for. Document how you used generative AI tools to produce or modify code or assist in other permitted tasks.
