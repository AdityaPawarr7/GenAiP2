## university_guidance
url: https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/technology/aitools/index.html
> The university’s policy on academic integrity is not changing as a response to generative AI tools. According to CMU’s Office of Community Standards and Integrity,

> already implicitly covers such tools, as they may be considered a type of unauthorized assistance. However, this policy is intentionally designed to allow instructors to define what is “authorized” vs. “unauthorized” and what constitutes “plagiarism” and “cheating.”

> If your policies are not explicitly stated, then your students may NOT be able to effectively interpret what kinds of AI use are authorized or not in your course

## intro_course
course: 15-112 Fundamentals of Programming, Fall 2026
url: https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~112/syllabus.html
> We embrace the importance of AI as a powerful tool, even as we also strongly resist AI replacing your learning or your humanity. AI use is allowed and even encouraged on preps, homeworks, labs, and projects. However, at no point can you use AI to replace your thinking or your understanding.

> All AI use is strictly forbidden on all assessments, including comps, quizzes, and exams.

course: 15-122 Principles of Imperative Computation, Fall 2026
url: https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~15122/syllabus.shtml
> You may not use AI tools for any part of work that you will submit for credit. This includes but is not limited to generating solutions, checking solutions, explaining tasks or solutions, formatting code, adding comments to code, or generating test cases. Use of these tools on assignments will be reported as an academic integrity violation.

> We require that you disable AI extensions from your programming environment and in general that you do not use AI tools.

## se_course
course: 17-313 Foundations of Software Engineering, Fall 2026
url: https://cmu-313.github.io/syllabus/
> You may use generative AI for any and all coding tasks in the course. However, as we discuss in class, we belive that there are significant implications for your own learning around your AI usage patterns. Our goal for you in this class is that you learn to use AI in a way that helps you become a better software engineer.

> We Very Strongly Discourage using AI to Generate writing responses, but we encourage its use for editing your writing.

> It is your responsibility to validate and verify AI-produced content before submitting it for class purposes.
