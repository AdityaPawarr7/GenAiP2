source_date: 2026 "Last Updated: August 24, 2026"
> Yes. Decisions about the use of AI in teaching are made by instructors and academic departments, consistent with Columbia's longstanding commitment to faculty governance, curricular autonomy, and academic freedom.

> Faculty can choose a course AI policy that allows unrestricted student use of AI, prohibits it entirely, or falls somewhere in between.

> recommends that faculty share clear expectations with their students about what constitutes appropriate (and inappropriate) use of AI tools in each of their courses.

> Faculty are discouraged from using AI detectors for the purpose of detecting academic integrity violations. Columbia's Center for Student Success and Intervention (CSSI) advises that AI detection software cannot be used as the sole form of evidence that a student inappropriately used Gen-AI on an assignment.
