## intro_course
course: CSCI 0111 Computing Foundations: Data, Fall 2026
url: https://cs111-docs.cs.brown.edu/s/syllabus-f26
source_date: 2026 "CSCI 0111 (Computing Foundations: Data): Syllabus, Fall 2026"
> Especially in an intro course, asking a generative AI tool (such as ChatGPT) to write your code for you does you a huge disservice.

> **Unless an assignment explicitly asks you to try an AI tool, using generative AI output that is beyond help that a TA would provide you is considered a violation of this policy.** For example, you can ask an LLM to rephrase a drill explanation for you, give feedback on steps of a plan before you start coding, or explain an error message to you (without rewriting your code); but **not** ask it to write code, tests, reflections, etc. for you.

> During the semester, we will have a balanced approach to AI. There are several activities you must do on your own without any AI assistance.

> Finally: at all times, unless we have explicitly given permission, any (suspected) use of an AI tool will be viewed as a violation of Academic Integrity
