## dept_statement
url: https://www.cs.umd.edu/class/resources/academicIntegrity.html
source_date: undated
> The CS Department takes academic integrity seriously. We expect all students to abide by the rules and regulations specified in the class syllabi and defined by the University of Maryland.

> The following are examples of academic integrity violations that apply to all our courses.

> Use of AI (Artificial Intelligence) tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Bing AI, etc.) for the completion of class work.

## university_guidance
url: https://ai-resources.umd.edu/guidelines.html
source_date: 2025 "Effective Date: 01/15/2025"
> Instructors are strongly encouraged to establish a course-specific policy that defines the appropriate and inappropriate use of GenAI tools. Students should assume that the use of GenAI tools to complete course assignments and assessments is not allowed unless otherwise specified in the course syllabus or assignment/assessment instructions.

> It will be at the course instructor's discretion to determine whether GenAI may be used, to what extent, and for which assignments and assessments.

url: https://tltc.umd.edu/sample-syllabus-language-ai-course-policies
source_date: undated
> The University of Maryland strongly encourages instructors to establish a course-specific policy that defines the appropriate and inappropriate use of Generative AI (GenAI) tools. The specifics of this policy are up to the instructor's discretion and depend on your course context and learning goals for your students.

## intro_course
course: CMSC 131 Object-Oriented Programming I (sections 03XX-04XX, Pedram Sadeghian), Fall 2026
url: https://www.cs.umd.edu/class/fall2026/cmsc131-03XX-04XX/
source_date: 2026 "CMSC131 Object-Oriented Programming I (Fall 2026)"
> All programming assignments in this course are to be written individually (unless explicitly indicated otherwise). Cooperation between students, using old projects from former students, using the web to find code, or AI use to develop your code, are all violations of the Code of Academic Integrity.

> Using any type of AI tool to create code for you such as chatGPT.

## se_course
course: CMSC 435 Software Engineering (Jim Purtilo), Fall 2026
url: https://seam.cs.umd.edu/purtilo/435/syllabus.html
source_date: undated
> Use of AI (e.g. ChatGPT, Claude or similar AI-inspired resources) without prior permission of the instructor is prohibited. We are chiefly interested in honing your skill sets - not a bot's.

> any graded work in which the solution approach involves use of AI outside the scope of the assignment will result in loss of up to 100 course points in addition to referral to OSC.

> Note that many of our projects will involve working with AI but the scope of how we do this will be explicit in the tasking statement.

> it must be approved in advance based on a written proposal, and then all prompting and results must become part of the project deliverable for our inspection.
